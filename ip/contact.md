# Zen Nova Solutions — contact.md

Full spec for the **Contact** page (linked from `index.md`) — the last of the five content files. Built on what makes contact pages actually convert: a form that sets expectations upfront, trust signals next to it (not buried in the footer), and more than one way to reach out for people who'd rather not fill out a form at all.

Colors, header, footer, and CTA pattern follow `index.md` — this file only covers what's unique to the Contact page.

---

## Design Principles Behind This Spec

1. **Two-column layout: info left, form right.** This is the standard that works because it removes uncertainty — the visitor sees who they're contacting *and* fills out the form in the same view, not one after the other.
2. **Set expectations before they submit.** "We reply within one business day" removes the anxiety of sending a message into a void — this line already exists on your homepage, and belongs here too, right next to the form.
3. **Action-oriented button text, not "Submit."** A generic label gives no reassurance about what happens next.
4. **More than one path.** Not everyone wants to fill out a form — WhatsApp and a direct phone number sit equally alongside it, not as an afterthought below.
5. **Trust signals live here, explicitly** — service area and location are already known; a couple of small gaps (business hours) are flagged below rather than guessed at.

## Contact FAQ

**Purpose:** Two questions a visitor has right before submitting a form — answering them here removes the last bit of hesitation.

- **How fast will I hear back?** Within one business day — often sooner if you reach out on WhatsApp during business hours.
- **Do I need to know exactly what I want before contacting you?** No — select "Not Sure Yet" in the form, or just message us directly. Most conversations start with figuring out what fits your business, not a finished brief.

## Summary of What Changed vs. the Current Site

- Restructured into an explicit **two-column layout** (info + trust signals left, form right) instead of contact details only appearing in the footer — the current site makes a visitor hunt for the phone number after they've already started filling out the form.
- Changed the form button from generic **"Send Message" to "Book My Free Consultation"** — ties the form's action to the same CTA language used everywhere else on the site, and tells the visitor what actually happens next.
- Added **expectation-setting microcopy** ("we reply within one business day") directly next to the form, not just on the homepage — this is proven to reduce hesitation right at the point of submission.
- Flagged **business hours** as a gap — not currently listed anywhere on the site, and one of the first things a local-business visitor looks for before calling or messaging.
- Added a **Contact FAQ** and an alternate **WhatsApp-first CTA** for visitors who'd rather not fill out a form at all — the current site only offers the form as a path in.
