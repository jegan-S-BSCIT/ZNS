## 10. FAQ

**How long does a website take?**
Most projects go live within days, not months — timelines depend on the plan and how quickly content and feedback come back from you, but we move fast without cutting corners.

**Do you redesign websites?**
Yes. If you already have a website that needs a fresh look, better performance, or a mobile-friendly rebuild, we can redesign it from the ground up.

**Can I update my website later?**
Yes. We can make updates for you after launch, and depending on the plan, we can also set things up so you're able to make simple content changes yourself.

**Do you provide support?**
Yes — every plan includes a support window after launch (7, 15, or 30 days depending on your plan), and you can always reach us directly on WhatsApp for anything after that.
