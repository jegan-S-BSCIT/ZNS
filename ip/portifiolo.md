# Zen Nova Solutions — portfolio.md

Full case-study spec for the **Portfolio** page (linked from `index.md`). Built on the "Challenge → Approach → Result" structure that case-study-driven portfolio pages use — a project screenshot alone doesn't tell a prospective client why it matters; the challenge it solved does.

Colors, header, footer, and CTA pattern follow `index.md` — this file only covers what's unique to the Portfolio page.

---

## Design Principles Behind This Spec

1. **Every project answers "what problem did this solve" before it shows off the visuals.** A screenshot is proof; the challenge is context.
2. **Consistent structure across all three case studies** — Challenge, Approach, Result, in that order every time — so visitors can compare projects and you can add new ones fast.
3. **No invented numbers.** Where a real result/metric isn't confirmed yet, this spec leaves a placeholder rather than a made-up stat — fill those in once you have real numbers from the client (enquiries, bookings, load time, whatever's actually true).
4. **Category tags, not just names** — helps a visitor scanning for "someone like me" (e.g. a retail shop owner looking for retail examples) find relevant proof fast.

## Case Study 1 — Urban Steps

**Category:** E-commerce · Footwear
**Project type:** Business Website Design + UI/UX Design

**Challenge:**
Urban Steps needed an online store where customers could browse footwear, pick the right size, and check out without confusion — the kind of friction that makes people abandon a cart on a cluttered e-commerce site.

**Approach:**
We designed a clean, mobile-first product grid with clear size selection built into the product page (not buried in a dropdown), and simplified the path from product page to checkout to as few steps as possible.

**Result:**
A footwear storefront that's simple to browse and buy from, on any device. *[Add a real outcome once available — e.g. checkout completion rate, mobile traffic share, or enquiry volume since launch.]*

**Visual note:** Full-width hero screenshot + 2–3 supporting screens (product grid, product detail, checkout).

## Case Study 2 — Saravana Studio

**Category:** Photography
**Project type:** Business Website Design + UI/UX Design

**Challenge:**
Saravana Studio's work — weddings, family portraits, birthdays — depends entirely on how it *looks* online. The site needed to feel as premium as the photography itself, without the gallery slowing the page down.

**Approach:**
We built an image-first layout that lets the photography carry the page, organized by shoot type (wedding, family, birthday, special occasions), with a booking-focused contact path so browsing turns into an enquiry.

**Result:**
An elegant, gallery-led site that presents Saravana Studio's work the way it deserves to be seen. *[Add a real outcome once available — e.g. booking enquiries since launch, average time on gallery pages.]*

**Visual note:** Large gallery-style screenshots — this project should feel the most visually led of the three.

**CTA under case study:** Want a Portfolio Site Like This? → Book Free Consultation

## Case Study 3 — Bharathi Phones & Frames

**Category:** Retail · Mobiles, Photo Frames & Gifts
**Project type:** Business Website Design

**Challenge:**
Bharathi Phones & Frames sells three fairly different product lines — mobiles and accessories, custom photo frames, and gifts/photography services — under one business. The challenge was presenting all three clearly without the site feeling scattered.

**Approach:**
We organized the site around clear categories so each product line has its own space, with consistent navigation tying them together as one coherent brand rather than three unrelated shops glued together.

**Result:**
One clean site that represents all three sides of the business without overwhelming a first-time visitor. *[Add a real outcome once available — e.g. enquiries per category, walk-in traffic influenced by the site.]*

**Visual note:** 3-panel or tabbed screenshot layout showing each product category.

**CTA under case study:** Want a Multi-Category Site Like This? → Book Free Consultation

## Summary of What Changed vs. the Current Site

- Each project now follows **Challenge → Approach → Result** instead of a single descriptive sentence — this is what turns a screenshot gallery into actual sales proof.
- Added **category tags** per project so visitors scanning for their own business type (retail, photography, e-commerce) can find relevant proof faster.
- Flagged every **Result** line as a placeholder for a real metric rather than inventing one — worth going back to these three clients for a real number (enquiries, bookings, whatever's true) as soon as you can.
- Added a **Portfolio FAQ** addressing the two questions a visitor silently has when a portfolio is only 3 projects: "is my industry represented" and "will my site actually be original."
