Design Notes
Typography: Use same font stack as index.md. Headings in bold, body text in regular weight for readability.

Spacing: Generous whitespace between sections — don't cram content. Each section should feel breathable.

Mobile: Stack all grids on mobile. Ensure team cards and values grid are readable on small screens.

Images: Use real photos if available (team, office, work-in-progress shots). If not, skip images rather than using generic stock.

Tone: Conversational, honest, grounded — no corporate jargon or inflated claims.

Content Principles Applied
This spec follows patterns from effective About pages at companies like Basecamp, Moz, and leading design studios:

Story before statistics — Open with why the studio exists, not a list of services.

Mission and values connect to the customer — "Honest Communication" means something only when tied to their experience (no surprise costs, clear updates).

Real people build trust — Named leadership with roles and photos consistently outperforms anonymous "our team" sections.

Keep it scroll-worthy, not exhaustive — This is the studio's story, not a resume — long enough to build trust, short enough to actually be read.

End with customer benefit — The last section before the CTA answers "Why should I care?" directly.
