# Zen Nova Solutions — Home Page Specification

A section-by-section spec: what each block is for, how it's laid out, and the copy to put in it. Built on patterns from higher-performing agency and small-business homepages — clear hero formula, early trust signals, outcome-framed proof, plain conversational tone, one CTA path repeated throughout.

---

## Design Principles Behind This Spec

1. **Hero states who you help and what changes for them** — not a mood line.
2. **Trust shows up early**, before the visitor has to scroll for it — but honestly. No invented client counts.
3. **Portfolio is framed around outcomes** ("what this got the business"), not just "here's a screenshot."
4. **One CTA path** ("Book Free Consultation") repeats at every natural exit point instead of competing CTAs.
5. **Plain, spoken tone** — short sentences, "you," no jargon.
6. **Testimonials slot is included even though you don't have any yet** — flagged clearly so you fill it in once you have 2–3 client quotes. This is one of the highest-leverage additions once available.

## Summary of What Changed vs. the Current Site

- Hero rewritten around a specific promise ("turn nearby customers into paying ones") instead of a generic "transform your brand" line.
- Added a **Trust Strip** right under the hero — currently the site asks visitors to scroll through 4+ sections before seeing any credibility signal.
- Added a **Testimonials slot** — flagged as your biggest missing element; don't fill it with fake quotes, but plan to add 2–3 real ones as soon as you have them.
- Portfolio blurbs reframed around outcomes rather than descriptions.
- Repeated one consistent CTA ("Book Free Consultation" / WhatsApp) at every section instead of leaving CTAs only at the top and bottom.
